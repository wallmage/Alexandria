## Part A — quotation gate, docs (worktree quote)

Files: `scripts/validate_report.py` (only `_QUOTE_SPAN_RE` and `_quoted_spans`), `tests/test_validate_report.py`, `SKILL.md`.

A1 Quote pairing (HARD `integrity/quotation-lost` on the model's own prose). `_QUOTE_SPAN_RE` (L102) matches ASCII quotes with `\"[^\"]{4,}\"`. Open and close are the same character, so a quoted term shorter than 4 chars (`"雪耻"`, `"共匪"`) fails the branch and the scanner restarts at that term's CLOSING quote, pairing it with the next term's opening quote; from there every stretch of prose between quotations is a "quoted span". Reproduction: `每日以"雪耻"开头。他写道："倭寇侮辱,非可以愤激制之"，此后转向强硬。` → current spans = `['"开头。他写道："']`; correct = `['"倭寇侮辱,非可以愤激制之"']`. On the real snapshot (`/Users/wallny/WorkBuddy/2026-09-15-20-23-53/report.pre-rewild.md`) 55 of 76 ASCII spans are bogus and all three HARD hits fell on prose the humanize step is allowed to edit.
Fix in `_quoted_spans`: pair ASCII quotes sequentially (`"[^"\n]*"`, any length, no newline inside) and keep a span only when its inner text is ≥ 4 chars; the `「」`, `『』`, `“”`, `‘’` branches stay exactly as they are (open ≠ close, no parity problem). Keep the function returning the full span including its quote characters, as today. Do not touch the other regex at L310.
Tests (append to `tests/test_validate_report.py`): (1) the reproduction string above as snapshot; editing the prose between the two quotes yields no `integrity/quotation-lost` finding; (2) same snapshot, altering the text inside `"倭寇…制之"` yields one `hard` finding; (3) `_quoted_spans('a "xy" b "long enough" c')` == `['"long enough"']`. Run the whole suite; `test_integrity_findings_lists_all_lost_quotes` and `test_altered_quotation_is_hard_and_a_removed_one_only_warns` must still pass unchanged.
